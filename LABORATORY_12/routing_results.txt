13. INVALID-INPUT HANDLING EVIDENCE
-----------------------------------
  [negative weight] rejected: negative edge weight -3 rejected: Dijkstra requires non-negative weights
  [non-unit weight on unweighted graph] rejected: unweighted graphs only accept unit weights (weight=1)
  [duplicate edge] rejected: duplicate edge 'A'-'B'
  Dijkstra requires non-negative weights because it settles a
  vertex permanently once its distance is the smallest in the
  queue; a later negative edge could lower that settled distance,
  so the greedy assumption would be false.
